Criteria Definitions i
Profit Factor 25%
Gross winning P&L divided by gross losing P&L. Above 1.0 means profitable overall; higher values indicate stronger edge. Score of 100 requires ≥ 2.6.
Avg Win / Loss 20%
Average winning trade divided by average losing trade. Measures how much you make on winners relative to what you lose on losers. Score of 100 requires ratio ≥ 2.6.
Max Drawdown 20%
Largest peak-to-trough decline in cumulative P&L, expressed as a percentage of the peak. Score = 100 − drawdown%. Lower drawdown = higher score.
Win % 15%
Percentage of trades that are winners. Scaled so that 60% win rate = score of 100. Values above 60% are capped at 100.
Recovery Factor 10%
Net profit divided by max drawdown (in dollars). Shows how efficiently you recover from losses. Score of 100 requires ≥ 3.5; below 1.0 scores 0.
Consistency 10%
Measures day-to-day performance stability. Calculated as 100 − (std dev of daily P&L ÷ total profit) × 100. Scores 0 if average daily P&L is negative.
